# NEXGEN Sentinel – Privacy Policy

**Effective Version:** 1.0.0
**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

**Developer:** NEXGEN DevStudio

## Summary

NEXGEN Sentinel is designed with a local-first privacy approach.

The extension analyzes information about installed browser extensions to provide security assessments, permission insights, health indicators, change detection, and recommendations.

Information analyzed by Sentinel remains locally within the browser unless otherwise stated in a future privacy update.

## Information We Access

Depending on the information made available by the browser, NEXGEN Sentinel may access information such as:

* Extension name and identifier
* Extension version
* Enabled or disabled status
* Extension type
* Installation information
* Declared permissions
* Declared website access permissions
* Basic extension metadata

This information is used only to provide Sentinel's features and security analysis.

## Website Content

NEXGEN Sentinel does not currently require access to the content of websites you visit.

The extension does not use website browsing activity for advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking.

## Data Storage

Sentinel may retain information necessary to provide features such as extension analysis, security assessments, and change history.

This information is kept locally within the browser and is not intentionally transmitted to NEXGEN DevStudio servers.

## Data Sharing

NEXGEN Sentinel does not intentionally sell, rent, or share analyzed extension information with third parties.

The released extension does not intentionally transmit analyzed extension information to external servers for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## Security Assessments

Sentinel's security scores, findings, risk indicators, and recommendations are informational.

They are based on information observable through supported browser APIs and do not guarantee that an extension is safe, unsafe, malicious, or free from security or privacy risks.

## Third-Party Services

NEXGEN Sentinel does not require an external account or remote service for its core functionality.

Third-party components, where included, remain subject to their applicable licenses and privacy terms.

## Privacy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when a future version of NEXGEN Sentinel materially changes the information it accesses, stores, or transmits.

The effective version and date will be updated accordingly.
